# Worked Examples — Community Post Ideas in Action

## Example 1: Personal-Finance Channel, One Week Requested

### Creator Input

```
Niche: Personal finance for people in their 20s — budgeting, saving,
first investments. Mostly talking-head videos with on-screen graphics.

Coming up: A video on high-yield savings accounts drops in 10 days.

Recent wins: My last video on budgeting apps hit 40K views, way above
my channel average.

Posting cadence: 4x a week (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

I only want this week's posts — 4 of them.
```

### Output

```
COMMUNITY POST CALENDAR — 4 DAYS (this week)

MONDAY — Poll
Post: "Working on the high-yield savings video and want your take:
should I lead with 'how much you're actually losing' by staying in a
0.01% APY account, or jump straight into the accounts I'd recommend?"
Options: A) The 'losing money' hook  B) Straight to recommendations
Best time: Monday morning — highest weekday activity
Expect: Votes + a few comments arguing for one option
Visual: None needed

WEDNESDAY — Behind-the-scenes
Post: "Spent this afternoon pulling current APY rates from 6 different
banks for the savings video. Two of them changed their rate THIS WEEK.
Trying to figure out how to make the video not go stale in a month —
open to ideas if you've got them."
Best time: Wednesday midday
Expect: Comments with suggestions, some genuine engagement on the
staleness problem
Visual: None needed

FRIDAY — Hot take
Post variation A: "Unpopular opinion: a 'high-yield' savings account
under 4% APY right now isn't actually high-yield anymore. It's just
average."
Post variation B: "If your 'high-yield' savings account is under 4%
right now, it's not high-yield — it's just keeping up with inflation.
Barely."
Best time: Friday morning — hot takes get more traction heading into
the weekend when people have time to argue in the comments
Expect: Comments, some disagreement, good for reach
Visual: None needed

SUNDAY — Content teaser
Post: "The savings account video drops in 3 days — this is the one
where I show exactly how much I moved from a 0.01% account to a 4.5%
one, and what that difference actually looks like after a year. Screenshot
included in the video, not just numbers on a slide."
Best time: Sunday evening — people planning their week, good time to
plant the reminder
Expect: A handful of 'can't wait' comments, sets up Wednesday's video
launch
Visual: A blurred/teased screenshot of the before/after balance
comparison would strengthen this one — worth the extra 5 minutes
```

## Example 2: Creator Gives No Channel Context

### Creator Input

```
Generate my community posts.
```

### Output

```
Happy to build your calendar — I need a few things first so this
isn't just generic filler:

1. What's your channel about, and what format are your videos usually
   (tutorials, vlogs, reviews, etc.)?
2. Anything coming up in the next 4-6 weeks — a video in production,
   a launch, an event — worth teasing in a post?
3. Any recent win or milestone worth mentioning (a video that did
   well, a subscriber count, something real)?
4. How many Community posts a week do you actually want? This defaults
   to daily, but most creators post 3-5x a week — let me know your
   real number so I don't build 30 days you won't use.

Once I have those, I'll put together your calendar.
```
