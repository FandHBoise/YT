# Community Post Ideas — Quick Start Guide

## What Is This?

Community Post Ideas generates a full month of YouTube Community tab posts — polls, questions, behind-the-scenes updates, hot takes — tied to your actual channel and upcoming content, instead of generic "hey everyone!" filler. Give it your niche, what's coming up, and your posting cadence, and it returns a day-by-day calendar with post copy, timing guidance, and expected engagement type.

## Who It's For

YouTubers who know the Community tab drives engagement but either forget to use it consistently or default to the same "new video is up" post every time. If you want a month of varied, specific posts ready to queue up in one sitting, this is built for that.

## Key Features

- **Real variety, not a rotating template** — 7 distinct post types (poll, question, behind-the-scenes, hot take, teaser, milestone, feedback ask), never repeated back-to-back
- **Tied to your actual channel** — polls and teasers reference your real upcoming videos and decisions, not placeholder topics
- **Timing and engagement guidance per post** — best day to post and what kind of response to expect (votes, comments, replies)
- **A/B phrasing for polls and hot takes** — 2-3 variations so you can test what lands with your specific audience
- **Matches your real posting cadence** — asks how often you actually want to post instead of assuming daily

## Example Use Case

You run a personal-finance channel and post 4x a week. You tell the generator your niche, that you have a video on high-yield savings accounts coming out in 10 days, and that your last video on budgeting apps did well. It returns 4 weeks of posts: polls testing which savings account feature to cover first, a behind-the-scenes post about researching APY rates, a hot take on a common budgeting myth, and a teaser for the savings account video timed 2 days before it drops — all in your voice, ready to copy into the Community tab.

## How to Install

1. Open your Claude project (or ChatGPT project/custom GPT)
2. Upload `SKILL.md` to your project knowledge base
3. Answer its questions about your channel and cadence to get your first calendar

## Try This First

### Full Month Calendar

```
Niche: [your channel's topic and format]
Coming up: [any videos/launches in the next 4-6 weeks worth teasing]
Recent wins: [a milestone, a video that did well, a real update]
Posting cadence: [how many Community posts per week you actually want]

Generate my 30-day Community post calendar.
```

### Just This Week

```
Same context as above, but I only want this week's posts — [N] of them.
```

### One-Off Post for a Specific Situation

```
I need a poll about [specific decision you're actually making — e.g.
two thumbnail options, a format change]. Give me 3 phrasing variations.
```

## Getting the Best Results

- **Bring one real, current detail.** A specific upcoming video topic or a genuine recent milestone makes every post in that week sharper — vague input produces vague output.
- **Confirm your real cadence up front.** If you post 3x a week, say so — a 30-day, 7-days-a-week calendar you'll never fully use isn't useful.
- **Use the A/B poll variations.** Post one phrasing, note which got more votes, and mention that pattern next time you generate a batch — the skill will lean into what's already working for your audience.

## What This Won't Do

This writes the post calendar and copy — it doesn't design poll graphics or accompanying images, schedule posts for you, or pull your actual Community tab analytics. It's built specifically for YouTube's Community tab; the cadence and format guidance is tuned for that surface, not for general social media posting.
