# Instagram Reels Script Writer

## What It Does

Writes complete, shot-ready Instagram Reels scripts for 15, 30, 60, or 90-second videos. You get a second-by-second script with visual cues, spoken words, on-screen text callouts, a post caption, and audio suggestions — all tailored to your niche, audience, and goal.

## Who It's For

Instagram creators who post Reels but spend too long scripting (or post unscripted and wonder why performance is inconsistent). Works for any niche: fitness, food, finance, lifestyle, business, education, beauty, and more.

## Key Features

- **8 proven hook types** — the skill picks the best one for your topic
- **Exact timing cues** — knows what needs to happen in the first 3 seconds
- **Visual direction included** — tells you what should be on screen, not just what to say
- **Caption included** — 150-300 words, first line optimized for "more" cutoff
- **Audio suggestions** — 3 specific trending audio recommendations per script
- **Goal-aligned CTAs** — different scripts for follows, saves, link-in-bio, or comments

## Example Use Case

You're a personal finance creator who wants to post a 30-second Reel about why people stay broke even when they earn decent money. You give the skill your topic, niche, and that your goal is saves. 90 seconds later you have a complete script starting with a contrarian hook ("Earning more money won't fix your finances"), 3 on-screen text callouts, the full caption, and audio suggestions.

## How to Install

### Claude Projects
1. Create a new Claude project for your Instagram content (or use an existing one)
2. Go to **Project Knowledge** and upload `SKILL.md`
3. Start chatting — describe your Reel topic and the script appears

### ChatGPT
1. Go to **My GPTs** → **Create**
2. Paste `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Name it "Reels Script Writer" and save
4. Use it for every Reel you plan

## Your First Prompt

```
Write me a 30-second Instagram Reel script.

Topic: 3 things I stopped buying that saved me $500/month
Niche: Personal finance for millennials
Goal: Saves
Format: Talking head
Audience: 25-35 year olds who feel like they're always broke despite decent income
CTA: Save for later
```

## What You Get Back

1. **Script header**: goal, format, and hook type chosen
2. **Second-by-second script**: every line timed, with visual direction
3. **Post caption**: hook line, body, 3-5 hashtags
4. **On-screen text list**: text overlays in order
5. **Audio suggestions**: 3 options that fit the vibe
6. **CTA placement**: where and how to deliver your call to action
7. **Pacing notes**: when to speed up, slow down, or pause for emphasis
8. **B-roll suggestions**: what to show when you're not on camera
