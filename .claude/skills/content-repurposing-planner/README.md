# Content Repurposing Planner — Quick Start Guide

Stop leaving content on the table. This skill turns one long-form piece into a full repurposing plan — specific formats, specific angles, specific briefs — so you know exactly what to make next.

---

## What Is This?

The Content Repurposing Planner is an AI skill that acts as your content strategist. Give it a YouTube video, podcast episode, blog post, or any long-form content, and it builds a concrete plan for repurposing it into 5-10 shorter pieces across your platforms.

It does not give you vague advice like "turn it into social posts." It tells you which moment to clip, what angle to use, where to post it, and how long it will take — so you can act on it immediately.

Best for:
- Solo creators who want to get more mileage from what they already made
- Teams planning their content calendar around a hero piece
- Anyone who produces long-form content but feels like their distribution is underperforming

## How to Install

### Claude (Recommended)

1. Open [Claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and go to **Projects**
2. Create a new project or open an existing one
3. Click **Add content** → **Add text**
4. Paste the full contents of `SKILL.md`
5. Save the project — the skill is now active for every conversation in this project

Every chat inside the project will use the Content Repurposing Planner by default. No need to re-paste instructions.

### ChatGPT

**Option A — Custom GPT:**
1. Go to [chatgpt.com/gpts](https://chatgpt.com/gpts) and click **Create**
2. In the **Instructions** field, paste the full contents of `SKILL.md`
3. Name it "Content Repurposing Planner" and save

**Option B — System prompt in a new chat:**
1. Start a new conversation
2. Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` as your first message, followed by: "Understood. Ready to build repurposing plans."
3. Then paste your content or describe your piece

## Your First 5 Minutes

Once installed, try one of these prompts to get your first repurposing plan:

**If you have a transcript or detailed description:**
> Here's my latest YouTube video about [topic]. [Paste transcript or detailed summary.] Build me a repurposing plan focused on Instagram and LinkedIn.

**If you want to describe it:**
> I just recorded a 20-minute podcast episode where I walked through how I went from 0 to 10,000 YouTube subscribers without running ads. My audience is beginner creators. I'm active on YouTube, Instagram, and I have a newsletter. What can I make from this?

**If you want to test it with a quick idea:**
> I made a video explaining the 3 biggest mistakes new podcasters make in their first 10 episodes. It's about 12 minutes long. What are the best repurposing options?

The skill will return a structured plan with specific pieces, angles, and a "Start here" shortlist.

## Tips for Best Results

**Give it the actual content, not just the topic.** The more specific you are about what you said, showed, or demonstrated, the more specific and useful the repurposing plan will be. Paste a transcript, drop in bullet points, or describe your key moments.

**Tell it your platforms.** The skill tailors formats to where you actually publish. If you're not on TikTok, it won't waste your time suggesting TikTok clips.

**Tell it your capacity.** Solo creator with 5 hours a week? Say so. The skill will prioritize accordingly and flag which pieces require heavy editing vs. which ones are quick wins.

**Use the "Start here" picks first.** The skill always surfaces the top 3 highest-impact pieces. Start there before working through the full list.

**Run it again with a different constraint.** If you want to see only newsletter ideas, or only short-form video options, just say so. The skill can narrow its focus.
