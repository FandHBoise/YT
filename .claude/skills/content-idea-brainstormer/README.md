# Content Idea Brainstormer — Quick Start Guide

## What Is This?

The Content Idea Brainstormer turns Claude (or ChatGPT) into your personal content strategist. Give it your niche, and it generates 10 specific, ready-to-film content ideas — each one with a working title, the angle that makes it interesting, and why it's likely to perform with your audience.

No more staring at a blank upload schedule. No more broad categories that leave you still stuck when you open the camera app. These are actual ideas you can film.

## What's Included

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `SKILL.md` | The core system — defines 7 idea types, output format, and quality standards |
| `README.md` | This guide — how to install and get started |
| `EXAMPLE.md` | Two fully worked examples showing the skill in action across different niches |

## How to Install

### Claude Projects
1. Open your Claude project (or create a new one)
2. Go to **Project Knowledge** in the left sidebar
3. Upload `SKILL.md` into the knowledge base
4. Start chatting — Claude is now your content strategist

### ChatGPT
1. Open ChatGPT and go to **Explore GPTs** > **Create**
2. Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Save your GPT and start generating ideas

## Your First 5 Minutes

Once installed, try one of these prompts:

### Quick Niche
```
I make personal finance content on YouTube. Give me 10 content ideas.
```

### With Audience Context
```
I'm a fitness creator targeting women in their 30s who are getting back
into working out after having kids. Give me 10 content ideas for YouTube.
```

### Platform-Specific
```
I post cooking content on TikTok — quick recipes, grocery hauls,
restaurant reviews. I want more ideas that are specifically good for TikTok's format.
```

### Specific Content Type
```
I run a personal development channel. I've been posting mostly tutorials
but I want to start mixing in more personal story content. Give me 10 ideas
weighted toward stories and behind-the-scenes.
```

## Tips for Best Results

- **More context = sharper ideas.** "Fitness content" is fine. "Calisthenics content for skinny guys aged 18-25 who want to build muscle without a gym" is better — the ideas will be noticeably more specific and more likely to actually perform.
- **Tell it what's already working.** If you've had a video blow up, mention it: "my best video was about X." The skill can use that as a signal for what your audience responds to.
- **Request a specific mix.** If you're running low on evergreen tutorial ideas, say so. If you need more personal/story content to balance a heavy tutorial schedule, ask for that. The skill can weight the list toward any idea type.
- **Use the Best Starting Point.** At the end of each list, the skill picks the one idea to film first and explains why. Don't skip this — it's the shortcut through decision paralysis.
- **Run it weekly.** Ten ideas covers roughly one to two weeks of content. Make this a recurring session: spend 5 minutes giving context, walk away with your next week's upload schedule figured out.
