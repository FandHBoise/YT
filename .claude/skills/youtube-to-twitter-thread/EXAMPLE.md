# Worked Examples — YouTube to Twitter Thread in Action

## Example 1: Editing Workflow Video, Full Transcript Provided

### Creator Input

```
Video title: I Cut My Editing Time in Half (Here's the Exact Workflow)

Transcript: [~2,800-word transcript covering: intro about editing taking
6+ hours per video, the switch to a proxy-footage workflow, setting up
keyboard shortcuts for the 5 tools used most, batching color grading
across all clips at once instead of per-clip, and a final result of
editing dropping from 6 hours to 2.5 hours per video]

Turn this into a Twitter thread.
```

### Output

```
THREAD (from: I Cut My Editing Time in Half (Here's the Exact Workflow)):

1/ I cut my editing time from 6 hours to 2.5 hours per video. Not with
faster hardware — with 3 workflow changes. Here's exactly what changed:

2/ First: I switched to editing on proxy footage instead of the raw
4K files. Timeline scrubbing went from laggy to instant, and I stopped
losing 20+ minutes a video just waiting for playback to catch up.

3/ Second: I mapped keyboard shortcuts to the 5 tools I actually use —
cut, ripple delete, blade, zoom, and the color wheel. No more mousing
to a toolbar for every single action.

4/ Third, and the biggest win: I stopped color grading clip by clip.
I batch it — cut the whole video first, then grade every clip in one
pass at the end. Way less back-and-forth.

5/ None of these are exotic. They're the boring stuff — proxies,
shortcuts, batching. But stacked together they cut a 6-hour edit
almost in half.

6/ That's the full workflow. Exact settings and a timeline walkthrough
in the video: [VIDEO LINK]

TWEET COUNT: 6
LONGEST TWEET: 267 characters
```

## Example 2: Creator Only Has a URL (No Transcript)

### Creator Input

```
Video title: My Camera Gear for 2026
Link: https://youtube.com/watch?v=example

Turn this into a Twitter thread.
```

### Output

```
I can't watch the video, so I can't pull specific moments from a link
alone — a thread built on just the title would be generic ("check out
my gear!") and wouldn't reflect what's actually in the video.

Can you paste the transcript (YouTube has a "Show transcript" option
under the video description — copy/paste works fine), or give me a
rundown of what you cover: which pieces of gear, in what order, and
any specific reason you switched to or recommend each one? Once I
have that, I'll turn it into a thread.
```
